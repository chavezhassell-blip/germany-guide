Destinations

Four regions, each worth its own trip. Combine them on a longer tour.

Bavaria and the Alps

Start in Munich, then take the train south to Neuschwanstein and the Zugspitze. Best from May to October.

  • Munich old town and markets
  • Neuschwanstein Castle, timed entry
  • Lake Eibsee walk

Berlin

A city of museums, memorials and late dinners. We pair guided history walks with free evenings.

  • Brandenburg Gate and the Wall Memorial
  • Museum Island
  • Kreuzberg food tour

The Rhine Valley

Over sixty castles line the Upper Middle Rhine. We base you in Bacharach or Rüdesheim.

  • River cruise, Koblenz to Bingen
  • Riesling tasting in the vineyards
  • Marksburg Castle visit

The Black Forest

Quiet trails, cuckoo clocks and cake. A good fit for families and slower travelers.

  • Triberg waterfalls
  • Freiburg old town
  • Titisee lake loop
